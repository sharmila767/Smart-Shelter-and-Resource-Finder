🗺️ Evacuation Guidance

Follow safe evacuation practices during floods, cyclones, earthquakes and other emergencies.

1️⃣ Stay Calm

Stay calm and listen to instructions from local emergency authorities.

2️⃣ Check Your Route

Follow official evacuation routes and avoid blocked or unsafe areas.

3️⃣ Carry Essentials

Take essential items such as water, food, medicines and important documents.

4️⃣ Move to Safety

Move towards an official emergency shelter or another safe location.

⚠️ Important

Always follow instructions from official disaster-management and emergency services.
